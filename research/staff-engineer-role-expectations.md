## Technical Requirements

### Languages & Runtimes
- **Java** — most common across listings (Visa, Okta, MongoDB, Acceldata)
- **Python** — ML/automation roles (Automation Anywhere, HackerRank)
- **Go (Golang)** — platform/infra roles (MatchMove, HackerRank, Enphase)
- **Node.js / TypeScript** — fullstack roles (Okta, ACV Auctions)
- **C++** — systems/performance roles (MatchMove, Yugabyte)

### Core Engineering
- **System Design** — HLD + LLD; every listing mentions it
- **Distributed Systems** — consensus, partitioning, replication, fault tolerance
- **Microservices Architecture** — service decomposition, inter-service comms
- **Data Structures & Algorithms** — expected at interview bar
- **Design Patterns** — SOLID, GoF, domain-driven design

### Cloud & Infrastructure
- **AWS / Azure / GCP** — at least one deep; multi-cloud preferred
- **Kubernetes** — container orchestration, Helm, operators
- **Docker** — containerization standard
- **CI/CD** — GitHub Actions (most common), Jenkins, GitLab CI
- **Infrastructure as Code** — Terraform, Ansible

### Databases
- **SQL** — PostgreSQL, MySQL, Oracle
- **NoSQL** — MongoDB, Cassandra, DynamoDB, Redis
- **Schema Design** — both relational and document/wide-column

### Observability & Operations
- Monitoring, alerting, SLOs, incident response
- Production on-call expectations
- Performance tuning, profiling

### Emerging (growing keyword presence)
- **AI/ML integration** — LLM APIs, AI agents, ML pipeline knowledge
- **Real-time systems** — event streaming, Kafka (inferred from dist-sys reqs)
- **Security** — OWASP, authentication/authorization, encryption

## Leadership & Behavioral Expectations

- **Technical leadership** — drive architecture decisions across teams
- **Mentorship** — code reviews, growth of senior ICs
- **Cross-functional collaboration** — product, design, business stakeholders
- **Org-wide influence** — engineering standards, tech talks, hiring bar
- **Ownership** — "you build it, you run it" culture
- **Communication** — articulate tradeoffs to non-technical audience

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **Java dominates** — 70%+ listings have it as primary. Python/Go are secondary but growing.
2. **System design is table stakes** — it's in every JD. Deep dist-sys fluency differentiates for the 50L+ band.
3. **Cloud-native** (K8s + one cloud provider) is baseline — not a differentiator anymore.
4. **80% of roles pay 25–50 LPA**; breaking into 50L+ requires product company or deep specialization.
5. **Services companies (Accenture, TCS, Infosys)** hire 3× more Staff Engineers than product companies but pay less.
6. **Hybrid/Remote is rare** — only 10% of listings. Most expect WFO.
7. **AI/ML keywords are filtering into Staff JDs** — not building models, but integrating them into platforms.

### What It Takes to Get 50L+

| Factor | 50-75 LPA | 75-100 LPA | 1 Cr+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Experience | 10-15 yrs | 10-16 yrs | 16-22 yrs |
| Primary skills | Java + Cloud | Multi-language + Deep platform | Leadership + Strategy |
| Company type | Product + MNC Services | Product companies | Director/VP roles |
| Differentiator | Distributed systems depth | Platform ownership | P&L / Org building |
| Work mode | Mostly WFO | WFO + some Hybrid | Mostly WFO |

**Key pattern:** The jump from 25-50 LPA (~7K jobs) to 50L+ (~1.2K jobs total) is filtered by:
1. **Product company** — services companies rarely pay 50L+ for Staff Engineer
2. **Deep platform/distributed systems** — not just CRUD, but infrastructure, storage, streaming
3. **Foreign MNC** — 440 out of 1,029 50-75L jobs are Foreign MNCs
4. **Java + Go/Python breadth** — single-language engineers get filtered out at 50L+
5. **Architecture ownership** — not just deliver features, own the platform roadmap
