## The 3 Rules of Negotiation

**Rule 1 — Never give the first number.** Whoever says a number first loses information. If you say ₹60 LPA, they might have budgeted ₹80 LPA. Always deflect.

**Rule 2 — Know your BATNA.** You are not desperate. You have a Staff Engineer role at Dish with awards, stability, and meaningful work. Internalize: *"I don't need this. I want this — but only on fair terms."*

**Rule 3 — Negotiate total comp, not just base.** Push base first, then bonus, then joining bonus, then RSUs, then learning budget and remote flexibility.

## Negotiation Scripts

### Stage 1: "What are your salary expectations?"

> *"I'm still evaluating the full scope of the role. Before I share expectations, could you tell me the compensation band you've budgeted? That would help me understand if we're in the same ballpark."*

**If they push:** *"I understand. Based on my research, Principal Engineer V roles at this level in Bengaluru typically range from ₹50-80 LPA total comp. But I'd rather hear what The Standard has budgeted."*

### Stage 2: "We're offering X"

**Pause for 3-5 seconds.** Do not fill the silence. Let them feel the gap. Then:

> *"I appreciate the offer. Based on my research, the market for a Principal Engineer with 14+ years, event-driven architecture at 10M+ subscriber scale, and AI-augmented SDLC is in the ₹60-70 LPA range for base. I'd be looking for a total package around ₹75 LPA to make this move compelling. Can we get closer to that?"*

**If they're low:** *"I'm interested, but this is below a lateral move from my current position. At Principal Engineer V, I'd expect base in the ₹60-65 LPA range. Can we bridge that gap through base, joining bonus, or RSUs?"*

### Stage 3: "Is that your final number?"

> *"I understand budget constraints. I'm genuinely excited about the role, but I need this to be a meaningful step forward from where I am today. If we can get to ₹65 LPA base with 15% bonus, I'd be ready to sign today."*

**Takeaway close (your strongest move):**
> *"If the budget genuinely can't flex to this level, I completely understand. I'll continue at Dish and we can revisit. But if there's room to move, I'd love to make this work."*

## What to Negotiate Beyond Base

| Item | Strategy | Ask |

## Red Flags

| Flag | Danger | Action |
|------|--------|--------|
| **Non-compete clause** | Legal hassle on exit | Ask to remove or narrow to "direct competitors you've worked on" |
| **Below-market (< ₹45 LPA base)** | They're hiring Principal for Senior money | Push back hard — 20-30% below market |
| **Vague bonus ("up to 20%")** | No guarantee | Ask: "What's on-target? What % hit target?" |
| **"We'll review in 6 months"** | Future promise, no guarantee | "I'd rather agree on the right number today. Otherwise, bridge with a signing bonus." |
| **No equity at Principal level** | No skin in the game | Push for LTI or cash retention bonus |
| **Unlimited vacation + no WFH** | Unlimited = fewer days taken | Ask for actual average days taken by engineers at this level |
