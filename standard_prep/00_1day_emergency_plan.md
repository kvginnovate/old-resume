## During the Interview — Emergency Signals

| Signal | Move |
|--------|------|
| Brain freeze | "That's a great question. Let me think about it for a moment." — Buys 10 seconds |
| Don't know the answer | "I don't have that specific experience, but here's how I'd approach it: [framework]" |
| Need time to think | Name the framework aloud: "Let me clarify the constraints first, then I'll walk through the architecture." |
| Caught off guard by a question | Bridge to a story you know: "That reminds me of a challenge I faced with the Sling TV migration..." |
| Technical question you can't answer | Be honest: "I haven't worked with that specific technology, but I can tell you how I'd evaluate it." |
| Interviewer seems disengaged | Ask a question back: "Does that align with a challenge you're facing?" |
