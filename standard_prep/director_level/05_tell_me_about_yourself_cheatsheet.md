## Delivery Rules (director-grade)

- **Swap verbs:** "I built / designed / implemented" → "I **chose / sequenced / influenced / de-risked**" — the staff-altitude tell
- **Pause after every number.** 10M. 99.9%. 70%. The numbers ARE the signal
- **No tech names until asked.** Litmus: can you say it to a VP of Product? If you need "Kafka" to explain why it matters, reframe
- **End every story with the business translation** — "unblocked releases", "features shipping again", never "lines migrated"
- **Watch for the hook.** MCP-in-Kiro / worktrees / GCP POC will trigger follow-ups — if they interrupt, stop and answer
- **2 min is the floor.** "Take your time" → expand to the 5-min version; otherwise stay tight
- **Never argue leveling.** Demonstrate altitude; the promotion + awards are the evidence
