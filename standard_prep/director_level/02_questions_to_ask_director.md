# Questions to Ask the Director — The Standard (Principal V)

> Pick **2-3**, adapt to the conversation, never read from a script. The closer is non-negotiable.
> Full list by interviewer type: `../15_questions_to_ask.md`. This file is the director-specific cut.

---

## The One Question — MUST ASK (closer)

**"What does success look like for this role in the first 6 months — what business outcome should I be directly responsible for?"**

- Director version: *"What business outcome should BenTech Integrations deliver that I'm directly responsible for by month 6?"*
- Shows outcome-orientation, reveals real expectations, calibrates the rest of the conversation.

---

## Director / VP Question Set

1. **"What's the technical vision for IBTE/BenTech Integrations in the next 3 years?"**
   - Coherent answer = strong leadership. Vague answer = you'll be inventing it yourself (important signal about the role's real scope).
2. **"How does BenTech Integrations fit into The Standard's broader insurance technology strategy?"**
   - Reveals whether this is strategic or a cost center — and where your work lands.
3. **"What's the biggest organizational challenge you're facing with engineering right now?"**
   - Tells you your non-technical job: talent, retention, culture, or process.
4. **"How are you sequencing the modernization of the legacy estate — strangler fig, greenfield services alongside the monolith, or specific domains carved out first?"**
   - Bridges to the Sling story; shows you've thought about the hardest part of legacy migration.
5. **"The JD mentions Apache Flink. What are the specific use cases — real-time claims, fraud detection, or something else?"**
   - Shows you noticed the unusual-in-insurance tech and understand stream-processing use cases in the domain.
6. **"How do you think about the organizational boundary between the India GCC and the US teams — fully autonomous product ownership, or integrated into US-led squads?"**
   - The CIO's model is "shared accountability"; this question shows operating-model thinking, not just tech.

---

## How to Read the Room

| Signal | Move |
|---|---|
| Technical conversation | Ask #4 or #5 (migration sequencing, Flink use cases) |
| Strategic conversation | Ask #1, #2, or #3 (vision, org challenges) |
| Warm conversationalist | Ask #6 (India-US operating model) — builds rapport |
| Interviewer wrapping up | The One Question. Clean, powerful closer |
| Rushed / late | Just The One Question and thanks |

**Never:** "I think you covered everything." Signals disengagement. Always have The One Question ready.

## Avoid in This Round

| Category | Example | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Too junior | "What tech stack do you use?" | You know it from the JD (Kafka/Confluent, Azure, Kong) |
| Too selfish | "What's the promotion path from Principal?" | Terminal role; save comp for HR |
| Too generic | "What's the culture like?" | Ask specifics about decision-making and autonomy |
| Negotiation | Salary / remote policy | Save for the offer stage |
