# 18. Interview Day Plan — The Standard (Bengaluru)

> Logistics, rituals, and contingency playbook for a hybrid Principal Engineer interview at The Standard, Bengaluru

---

## Week Before

- Re-read all 17 prep files, one per evening.
- Tech audit (remote): test camera, mic, internet on a 15-min Zoom call. Face a window for light.
- In-person: confirm office address, gate, floor, parking, visitor badge. Documents: photocopy Aadhaar, PAN, pay slips, offer letter, ID card. Keep originals + copies in one folder.
- Clothes: full-sleeve shirt, pressed trousers, closed shoes. Try on. No checks/ties.
- Sleep: shift bedtime 30 min earlier each night. Target 7.5 hours.
- Mock: 1 peer mock. Focus on Principal questions: org-wide influence, incident leadership, trade-offs.
- Review: 3 anchor stories, system design heuristics, Java/Spring Boot, SQL patterns, Dish metrics.

---

## Day Before

- **Tech** (remote): 1080p camera at eye level. USB mic. Window or ring light at 45°, no backlight. Wired internet > 5 GHz > 2.4 GHz, test ≥25 Mbps. 4G hotspot backup. Install Zoom/Teams. Test screen sharing.
- **In-person**: Google Maps to The Standard Bengaluru. Estimate 8 AM and 2 PM. Book cab — auto/metro backup. Confirm parking.
- **Evening**: light dinner by 7:30 PM. No alcohol/caffeine after 4 PM. Pack: laptop (charged), charger, headphones, water, documents, pen, notebook, power bank, mints. Lights out by 10:30 PM.

---

## Morning Of

- **Breakfast**: 2 hours before first round. Complex carbs + protein (oats + eggs). No heavy/sugary/new food.
- **Coffee**: 1 cup, 90 min before start. **Water**: 500 ml on waking, 250 ml/hour. Stop 30 min before each round.
- **Warm-up** (30 min): 5 min box breathing (4-4-4-4), 5 min visualization, 10 min skim anchor stories, 10 min silence. No phone.

---

## During — 5-Minute Pre-Round Ritual

Before EVERY round (remote and in-person):

1. **Deep breathing** (60s): inhale 4s, hold 4s, exhale 6s. x4.
2. **Visualization** (60s): "I am prepared. I belong at this table."
3. **Open body language** (30s): shoulders back, chin up, palms open. Smile.
4. **Materials check** (30s): notebook, pen, water, screen shared.
5. **Grounding** (60s): 5 things you see, 4 feel, 3 hear, 2 smell, 1 taste.

Enter 2 min early. Greet warmly. Use interviewer's name.

**During round**: pause 2s before answering. Structure answers. Don't bluff. Jot questions. Signal at 5 min left.

---

## Between Rounds

1. Hydrate + bio break. 2. Review last round notes (5 min). 3. Skim next round prep (5 min). 4. Do 5-min ritual. 5. Enter 2 min early.

**Do NOT** replay the last round, check phone, talk to other candidates, or eat heavy. Banana or almonds max.

**Remote**: same seat/setup. Camera off, mute. Stand and stretch. **In-person**: stay in waiting area. Documents on you. Water only.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Action |
|---|---|
| Camera/mic fails | Re-plug or switch to phone. Share number in chat. |
| Internet drops | Hotspot. Rejoin within 2 min. Call interviewer. |
| Power outage | UPS. If none, call recruiter to reschedule. |
| Blank on question | "Let me think." Sip water. Restate. Scaffold from first principles. Ask to come back. |
| Disagree with interviewer | "I see it differently — can you help me understand the gap?" Never argue. If hostile, stay calm, redirect. |
| Nervous | Slow down. Slower speech sounds confident. Breathe. |

---

## After

- **Self-assessment** (30 min): log rounds, interviewers, what went well (2-3), what to improve (1-2), questions asked.
- **Follow-up email**: Hi [Recruiter], thank you for coordinating today. I enjoyed the conversations, especially [topic]. Looking forward to next steps. Best, Chokkar.
- **Next day**: another round? Review log, prep weak spots. No feedback? Wait 3-5 business days.
- **Log the experience**: company, role, rounds, questions, self-rating (1-5). Compounds over time.
- **Notes**: what question types did The Standard emphasize? What was the bar? Write down surprise questions verbatim.
