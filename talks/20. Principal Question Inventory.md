## 4. Technical Strategy & Governance (Principal-signature area)

- 🔍 **How do you define a long-term technical strategy that spans multiple teams or the entire organization?**
- 🔍 **Describe a time you identified a systemic architectural deficiency. What was your strategy to address it, and what was the outcome?**

- 🔍 **What governance mechanisms do you use to ensure architectural alignment across teams?** (architecture review boards, tech radar, ADRs, architecture champions)
- 🔍 **How do you align engineering goals with company strategy, and how does that cascade into team OKRs?**
- 🔍 **How do you handle risk management for foundational platform changes — backward compatibility, migration plans, rollback strategies?** (your parallel-run UMS story is the answer)
- 🔍 **How do you evolve an API or platform without breaking existing consumers?** (versioning, contract testing, deprecation policy)
- 🔍 **What metrics do you track for a platform you own? How do you balance velocity with stability?** (error budget, SLOs, DORA)
- 🔍 **How do you run post-mortems so that the learnings actually spread across teams?**

## 8. Situational / First 90 Days (high probability)

- 🔍 **Walk me through your ideal first 90 days as a principal engineer here.** (month 1: learn systems/people/priorities; month 2: ship something small end-to-end; month 3: identify a real improvement and ship it. NEVER promise to "transform" anything)
- 🔍 **You join our team and notice something isn't working well. What do you do in your first few weeks?** (listen first, understand why it's that way, small shippable change to build trust, buy-in before sweeping changes)
- 🔍 **Something breaks in production on a Friday afternoon and you are the most senior person online. What happens next?** (assess severity → communicate immediately → contain → debug → post-mortem write-up)
- 🔍 **Your team committed to a deadline that now looks unrealistic. How do you handle it?** (flag early, cut/defer scope, honest stakeholder conversation, revised plan — never "I'll just work harder")
- 🔍 **You need to choose between two technologies for a new project. How do you make that call?** (decision framework: team familiarity, maintenance, performance needs, timeline — they test reasoning, not the pick)
