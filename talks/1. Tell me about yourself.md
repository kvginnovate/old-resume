# 1. Tell Me About Yourself

**Target:** Principal Software Engineer V — The Standard (IBTE)
**Duration:** 75-80 seconds

---

## Answer

I'm a Staff Engineer at Dish Network Technologies with 14 years of experience in backend architecture and microservices.

Currently, I own the end to end architecture for the subscriber facing platform, which is built on Java and Spring Boot microservices serving 10 million subscribers with 99.9 percent uptime on Kubernetes. I define architecture patterns, lead design reviews, drive technical direction, and stay actively involved in code reviews and implementation.

One of my biggest contributions was introducing AI spec driven modernization at Dish. I built a workflow that uses AI agents to reverse engineer legacy code, generate specifications, and automate the remediation of breaking changes, turning what used to be months of manual migration work into an automated process. This approach was adopted company wide across teams, and we applied it to migrate 80 plus legacy Ruby on Rails APIs to Java microservices, cutting delivery timelines by 70 percent.

I've also won back to back hackathons building tools for HR and operations teams that were adopted across the organization.

I received the Annual Technical Excellence Award in 2024 and 2025 for my contributions to the platform.

Before Dish, I spent 10 years at MSys Technologies progressing from Software Engineer to Technical Architect, building enterprise platforms for clients like Pivot3 and NetApp.

I'm here because this Principal role is about architecture ownership and org wide standards, which is exactly what I've been doing and what I want to do more of.
