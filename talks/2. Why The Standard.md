# 3. Why The Standard

**Target:** Principal Software Engineer V — The Standard
**Duration:** 60-70 seconds

---

## Answer

That's a fair question. I've had a great run at Dish — promoted to Staff Engineer, back-to-back Technical Excellence Awards, built things adopted org-wide. I'm proud of that work.

But I've reached a point where I want broader scope. At Dish, I own the architecture for the subscriber platform and Sling TV. It's one product line. I'm ready for a role where I drive technical strategy across multiple product lines — insurance, retirement, investments — each with its own domain complexity.

What drew me to The Standard is three things. First, the Bengaluru centre is brand new — this is a founding architecture role. I get to define how things are built from day one, not inherit someone else's decisions.

Second, insurance is a natural fit for event-driven architecture. Claims, underwriting, policy management — these are textbook event-driven workflows. I've been doing exactly that with Kafka at Dish, and I want to double down on it at a Principal level.

And third, I read the CIO's interview where he said The Standard is focused on solving the hardest problems of AI first — underwriting, claims, integration — not chatbots. That's exactly the kind of real AI work I've been building. I could have joined any Bangalore GCC, but I chose The Standard because the technical challenge is real and the leadership is clear about what they want to build.

- Bridges directly to existing experience (Kafka, AI agent work)
- Intentional choice — not just another job application

## Notes

- Do not say "IBTE" — the interviewer knows their own acronyms. Keep it conversational.
- Do not say "I'm leaving because..." — say "I'm looking because..." Subtle reframe, important.
- The closer line ("I could have joined any Bangalore GCC, but I chose The Standard") is the power move. Deliver it with conviction, not as a throwaway.
- If asked about Azure/AKS gap: frame as "Kubernetes is Kubernetes, cloud patterns transfer. I've studied Azure specifically for this role."
- Do not complain about Dish. Frame everything as growth, not dissatisfaction.
