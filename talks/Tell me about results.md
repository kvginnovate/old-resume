## Answer

One of the most tangible results I delivered was building an Asset Management system during the Codefest 2023 hackathon and getting it adopted company wide.

I didn't just build the application. I containerized it with Docker, set up Portainer on internal VMs, and deployed it through JFrog. That full pipeline is what made it accessible to everyone — any team could spin it up without manual setup. It automated what was previously a manual tracking process and cut effort by 70 percent.

The same approach was used for the Shift Allowance portal, which automated long standing manual Excel workflows for HR and payroll. It earned a Spot Award and is now used company wide by every DISH employee.

The pattern across both is the same — identify a manual process, build a full stack solution, and make it deployable and operational so it scales beyond my own team.
